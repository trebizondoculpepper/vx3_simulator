# Safety signage

Real German safety signs, posters and photographs for the lab's walls, downloaded from Wikimedia Commons on 13 October 2026 by `fetch.py` (run `python3 -I fetch.py <dir>` to fetch them again). Every file below has a licence that lets it ship in the game. The SVGs are the originals; the PNGs beside them are 1600-pixel renders from Commons, ready to use as textures.

- `iso-7010/`: today's signs (DIN EN ISO 7010, used in Germany under ASR A1.3), named by code: rescue (E), fire (F), warning (W), mandatory (M) and prohibition (P). Public domain. Fits the setting's modern safety signs on old equipment. W006 (magnetic field), P007 (no pacemakers) and P014 (no metal implants) suit the ferrocores.
- `ghs/`: the GHS hazard pictograms used on chemical containers and in German Betriebsanweisungen. Public domain.
- `gdr-tgl-30817/`: the GDR's own safety signs (TGL 30817, 1986): A prohibition, B mandatory and escape, C warning, D first aid and fire (on blue). Public domain. Suited to signs left on the old GDR apparatus.
- `posters/`: notices and infographics: fire classes, model escape plans (Flucht- und Rettungsplan, DIN ISO 23601), photographs of the Brandschutzordnung Teil A notice, alarm call-point plates, a gas main-valve plate, the BMZ (fire panel) sign, the GDR exit and extinguisher signs and the old road sign for first aid.
- `photos/`: real signs as fitted, for reference on wear, mounting and placement.
- `reference/`: the DGUV first-aid poster (DGUV Information 204-001), the notice every German workplace must display. It is **copyrighted and only for reference**: draw our own version from it, and do not ship it in the game or the repository.

Licences that require attribution (CC BY, CC BY-SA, GFDL) must be credited if the file ships; CC BY-SA also applies to derived textures.
