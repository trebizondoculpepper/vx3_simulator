# VX Simulator '97 marketing art

The flat cover, alternate cover and big-box mockup are original promotional concepts in an early-1990s airbrush style. They depict the fictional VX rig and are not gameplay captures.

The six `screenshot-*.png` files are captures of the running simulator with the room lights on and the on-screen control layer hidden: the full VX3 rig, the 1986 control console both cold and active, the BBC Micro bench, the xenon bank, and the plant-room entrance. The active-console shot stages a running ferrocore state through the development inspection hook with reduced motion enabled; the other captures show the ordinary cold scene. These are game renders rather than the painted cover's imagined detail.

Use `vx-simulator-97-cover.png` as the primary front-cover concept and `vx-simulator-97-box-mockup.png` for a period retail-box presentation. The alternate cover is a second composition for comparison.

## Two-scientist box concepts

`vx-simulator-97-front-scientists-a.png` and `vx-simulator-97-back-scientists-a.png` form the first front/back pair, based on the earlier scientist cover. `vx-simulator-97-front-scientists-b.png` and `vx-simulator-97-back-scientists-b.png` form a second pair based on the original equipment-led cover. Both fronts show two scientists collaborating over a live rig problem. Both backs use the existing game captures and period-style aspirational copy, with a fictional "Available for PC and Commodore Amiga" platform line. These are packaging concepts; the platform line is part of the imagined 1997 release, not a statement about actual builds.

The four new panels were made with the built-in image generation tool. The front prompts retained the source cover's chrome title, rack, palette and painted style, added two collaborating scientists, and removed ironic or retrospective copy. The back prompts used the matching front plus the actual rack, console and laboratory screenshots as references, asked for faithful screenshot insets, and specified the headline, feature copy and platform line verbatim. The original assets remain available for comparison.

`vx-simulator-97-box-scientists-a-front.png` and `vx-simulator-97-box-scientists-a-back.png` present design A as front and reverse views of a physical 1990s cardboard software box. The built-in image generation prompts used the flat A panels as wrap references and requested a consistent spine, box thickness, studio lighting and modest shelf wear.
