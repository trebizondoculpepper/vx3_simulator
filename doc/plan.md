# VX3 Simulator plan

## Current slice

The workstation presents a fixed six-section early-1970s VX3 rack in a low-resolution science laboratory. Each section uses community apparatus and manufacturer names where research supports them. Individual maker liveries vary between rainbow, beige, bold blocks, industrial chevrons and camouflage, all behind the hardware. The rack has switches, knobs, a band selector and pushbuttons; analogue gauges, scope and counter; orbiting perspective and isometric views; zoom and pan controls; and an operator log. A six-position ferrocore drum and a second cabinet of amplifier, emitter and radiator gear sit to the right. A filing cabinet opens operating papers, and period advertisements hang above the safety board. The widened room has a proper right-hand wall carrying an idle, bracket-mounted teleprinter, with fanfold stock below and spent output piled in front; the camera can pan far enough to inspect it. A brass pedestal ashtray stands left of the xenon cylinders. Below the flux map, a load and kWh meter and a pulsing Time Dilation crystal report network outputs. Instrument placement reserves clearance beside the rack handles. Procedural audio follows controls and rig state, including the Encabulator motor and rotation pulse. Vertical scrolling moves the camera in depth, Option/Alt plus scrolling moves it in height, and horizontal mouse swipes pan sideways.
