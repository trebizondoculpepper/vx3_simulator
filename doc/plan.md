# VX3 Simulator plan

## Current slice

Build a scrollable 3D rack with a mains panel, valve bank, spindle drive, field regulator and monitor, assembled from a small catalogue of deliberately invented panel variants. Give each panel rack ears, bolts and its own finish. Place working switches and knobs on the panels, feed them into the existing simulation, and show readings on the instruments. Record operator actions and rig events in a dismissible notebook overlay. Offer perspective and isometric camera views for the owner to compare.

## Next

Use the pending lore research to expand the catalogue while marking community and invented material clearly. Add further instruments, patching and audio, then refine the camera and interaction feel after playing the first slice in a browser.
