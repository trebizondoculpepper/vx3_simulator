# VX3 Simulator plan

## Current slice

The workstation presents a fixed six-section early-1970s VX3 rack in a low-resolution science laboratory. Each section uses community apparatus and manufacturer names where research supports them. Individual maker liveries vary between rainbow, beige, bold blocks, industrial chevrons and camouflage, all behind the hardware. The rack has switches, knobs, a band selector and pushbuttons; analogue gauges, scope and counter; orbiting perspective and isometric views; zoom and pan controls; and an operator log. A six-position ferrocore drum and a second cabinet of amplifier, emitter and radiator gear sit to the right. A filing cabinet opens operating papers, and period advertisements hang above the safety board. Procedural audio follows controls and rig state, including the Encabulator motor and rotation pulse. Horizontal mouse swipes pan the camera.
