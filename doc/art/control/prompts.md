## The Grand Switchboard

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: environment concept art for VX3 Simulator, a PS1-era industrial laboratory game. Create a landscape, detailed painterly 3D game environment concept, readable hard-surface forms with subtly low polygon geometry and restrained texture grain. Eye-level wide three-quarter view from the west workbench toward the EAST wall of a modest rectangular basement lab. The whole east wall is dominated by a vast built-in industrial control installation, from near floor level to near ceiling, almost the full length of the wall, with shallow control desks projecting into the room. A corner of the worn wooden workbench appears in the bottom left foreground, opposite the panel. Grey-green plaster walls, concrete tiled floor, exposed overhead conduits and suspended fluorescent fixtures. Human-scale believable hardware. This is an original fictional installation inspired by real power-station control rooms, not a replica or disaster scene. No people, no logos, no flags, no radiation symbols, no giant modern flat screens, no science-fiction holograms. Image only, no collage, no title typography. Direction: Battersea-inspired analogue grandeur adapted to a utilitarian Soviet laboratory. Long imposing symmetrical bank of warm ivory enamel switchboards divided by dark steel uprights, black upper bands with finely drawn white electrical mimic diagrams, large circular cream-faced meters with heavy black bezels, a few oversized octagonal synchroscopes, orderly black Bakelite rotary handles, engraved label plates and brass screw heads. Continuous shallow angled dark green operator ledge at waist height, closed steel cupboards below. Six broad cabinet bays with repeating meter groups and a prominent central master instrument. Muted cream, charcoal, oxidised brass and bottle-green palette. Cool fluorescent room lighting with warm highlights on the instruments. Strong ordered rhythm, monumental but tactile. Entire installation visible from end to end.
