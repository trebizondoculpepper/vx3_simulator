# VX3 wall poster record

These three portrait advertisements were generated with the built-in imagegen tool on 2026-10-11 for the reconstructed laboratory wall. The brand campaigns, equipment depictions, typography, copy and model are original advertising fiction; they are not historical community artifacts. The images are installed at `public/posters/volt-xoccula.png`, `public/posters/neumann-dynamik.png` and `public/posters/bonk-industries.png`.

## Volt Xoccula

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: portrait 1970s promotional poster texture for a fictional VX laboratory in a low-poly PS1-style browser game. Primary request: a period 1973 Volt Xoccula print advertisement for full VX3 rack equipment, with an incongruously glamorous adult woman posing beside the tall analog rack, clearly a staged ad model rather than an operator. Style: screen-printed 1970s European science trade-fair poster, halftone grain, offset ink registration, bold geometric rainbow curves, cream paper, orange, cyan and vermilion. Composition: vertical poster, large illustrated rack with knobs, meters and vacuum tubes, woman in modest period fashion with bell sleeves, warm smile, no sexualization. Clear exact headline at top: 'VOLT XOCCULA'. Smaller exact bottom line: 'SCIENCE, MADE DELTA'. No other lettering, no logos from real companies, no watermark. Make the poster readable at small size and visually distinct from a modern photo.

## Neumann Dynamik

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: portrait 1970s promotional poster texture displayed high on a wall in a fictional VX science lab, to be readable at small scale. Primary request: Neumann Dynamik trade advertisement for a seven-stage betaform amplifier and matching rack-mounted VX laboratory equipment. Style: sophisticated 1974 German industrial graphic design, offset lithography, aged beige paper, restrained teal, ochre and black, precise geometric circles and grids, editorial illustration of an analog instrument cabinet with seven glowing stages, knobs and meter. Composition: vertical poster with strong framing and negative space. Exact large headline: 'NEUMANN DYNAMIK'. Exact short line at bottom: 'PRECISION IN EVERY STAGE'. No people in this poster. No other lettering, no watermark. The equipment and typography should feel distinctly different from a rainbow Volt Xoccula campaign.

## Bonk Industries

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: portrait 1970s promotional poster texture for a fictional VX science laboratory wall, readable at small size. Primary request: a 1976 Bonk Industries advertisement for an Encabulator drive and rotating ferrocore drum beside rack-mounted laboratory equipment. Style: exuberant mid-1970s industrial trade-show print, coarse halftone, screen-print texture, acid yellow, safety orange, chocolate brown, black chevrons and starburst shapes on aged paper. Composition: vertical poster, dominant angular illustration of the drum and rack, bold simple headline at top exactly 'BONK INDUSTRIES', short footer exactly 'PUT MOTION TO WORK'. No person in this poster. No additional lettering, no watermark. Clearly different from a restrained beige Neumann technical advert and a rainbow Volt Xoccula advert.
