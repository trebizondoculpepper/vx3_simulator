# Panel legend audit

The ferrocore rig previously replaced the intended silk-screen and Dymo wording with numbered LABEL placeholders. The shared renderer now prints the intended wording in both the lab and the dev copy. Missing captions were added beside channel selectors/fault lamps, cathode and beam-current meters, evaporator setpoints/readouts, supply trim, core temperature/pressure/xenon readouts, the fore-vacuum gauge, beat-frequency trimmers, cabinet voltage and its adjustment. Unwired inputs retain plausible duties and their log names derive from the printed legends; they remain marked as spare inputs in the diagnostic log.

The isolator's maker is NEUMANN, matching the existing Neumann Dynamik station rather than the LABEL placeholder. The development control board now carries German technical and operating legends: STOP, QUITT, FREI, SPÜL, PRÜF, RÜCKS., KATODENSTROM, VORVAKUUM, FLUSS, SCHWEBUNG and LABORLICHT, with hardware-type captions retained in German for auditioning. Its sampled displays are explicitly a FC-PRÜFSTAND. The cylinder-bank flow caption is Xe FLUSS. Existing east-board, recirculator, mercury rectifier, gas-warning, BBC keyboard, monitor and archived first-rig legends already have apparatus-specific wording.

These panel designs and maker pairings remain local inventions in the register described by doc/lore/summary.md. This audit adds names and visible legends to unwired controls without claiming new machine behaviour.

The corridor cheat board now offers STAGE 3 COMPLETE (plant supply and recirculator on, all column supply selections on, cold core sealed in a purged vessel at 1.8 bar, inlet open and outlet shut, drive breaker off) and LIGHTS ON (both lab and plant room). XENON ON also starts the recirculator and its supply. The Stage 3 shortcut implements the end of section 3 of doc/runbooks/cold_start.md; the next operator step is the drive's NETZ handle.

The mercury rectifier's arc and room illumination pulse together at eight flashes per second when powered; reduced motion keeps the light steady. The plant-light layer and its shadows continue to isolate it from the lab. Original poster and BBC-guide PNG artwork is retained; the game uses smaller WebP copies decoded into canvas textures before GPU upload, matching the rendering path used by the other printed panels. Temporary title-card fallbacks were removed.

The southmost CRT now shows pre-rendered green dimensional projections keyed to the live ferrocore temperature, with the critical boundary at the model's 3,200 K full-power threshold and a fault display when the core fails. Assets and a six-screen contact sheet are under public/screens/criticality/. Corridor lighting is permanently on and isolated from the two room circuits.
