# BBC Micro boot sound

`bbc-model-b-boot.wav` is the two-second interval from 00:03 to 00:05 of the [BBC Micro boot reference supplied by the owner](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s51kxN6k-zs&t=3s), included at the owner's explicit request. It is a mono 22,050 Hz PCM WAV with a 5 ms fade in and 15 ms fade out to prevent edit clicks. No other portion of the video is included. It plays once on computer reset, replaces any previous boot playback, follows the sound volume/mute controls and originates at the bench computer in the spatial mix. The other apparatus sounds are synthesised.
