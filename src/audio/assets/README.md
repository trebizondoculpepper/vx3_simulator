# Audio asset record

`bbc-model-b-boot.wav` is the two-second interval from 00:03 to 00:05 of the [BBC Micro boot reference supplied by the owner](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s51kxN6k-zs&t=3s), included at the owner's explicit request. It is a mono 22,050 Hz PCM WAV with a 5 ms fade in and 15 ms fade out to prevent edit clicks. No other portion of the video is included. It plays once on computer reset, replaces any previous boot playback, follows the sound volume/mute controls and originates at the bench computer in the spatial mix.

The cassette player keys use recordings by Joseph Sardin from BigSoundBank, published under CC0: [Cassette Player: Play and Pause](https://bigsoundbank.com/cassette-player-play-and-pause-s0570.html) supplies `cassette-play.mp3` and `cassette-stop.mp3`; [Cassette Player: Rewind and Stop](https://bigsoundbank.com/cassette-player-rewind-and-stop-s0566.html) supplies `cassette-fast-forward.mp3`. These are short mono excerpts, trimmed and level-adjusted for the three mechanical keys. The Fast Forward cue uses the mechanically similar rewind key press, not a recording of the motor running forward. The rest of the apparatus effects are synthesised.
