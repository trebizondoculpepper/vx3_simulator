# Audio credits

This is the central credit record for VX3 Simulator's sound effects and music. New recordings should list the author (with a profile or website where available), the original source page, the licence stated there, the local filenames and any edits. Keep technical encoding notes in the audio asset record.

## Procedural effects

Control clicks, electrical hum, rectifier buzz, hydraulic head movement, clamp and end-stop impacts, purge hiss, the ferrocore's temperature/spin-dependent voice and its burnout/flux-collapse sounds are original procedural patches in the [VX3 Simulator source](src/audio/). They use Web Audio and [ZzFX](https://github.com/KilledByAPixel/ZzFX), by [Frank Force](https://github.com/KilledByAPixel), MIT. They contain no external apparatus recording.

## Cassette music

Morgenstrom, Fernverbindung, Leichte Arbeit and Offener Horizont are original tracks generated locally for this project with [ACE-Step 1.5](https://github.com/ace-step/ACE-Step-1.5). The [cassette record](public/music/README.md) documents the exact prompts, model/checkpoint, seeds, generation date, measured loop points, audio processing and licence information found for the generator. Stylistic references were not sampled or fed to the generator.

Panel-button and door-latch presses use a 0.14-second mechanical click edited from the existing cassette-play recording by [Joseph Sardin](https://josephsardin.fr), [Cassette tape recorder: Play, Stop — sound 0570](https://bigsoundbank.com/cassette-tape-recorder-play-stop-s0570.html), CC0. The excerpt is filtered, faded and mixed quietly; it replaces the electronic button tone.

Switch and rocker defaults use two edited clicks from [ROCKER SWITCH.wav](https://freesound.org/people/StevenBrown/sounds/128433/) by [StevenBrown](https://freesound.org/people/StevenBrown/), licensed [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). The high-quality MP3 preview was trimmed at 3.08 seconds (0.15-second switch click) and 5.38 seconds (0.20-second rocker click), filtered, faded, peak-normalised and converted to mono PCM16 WAV. The rocker retains a deeper body; both defaults are shared by the lab and the dev-room control test.

Keycaps use a mechanical keyboard press from [Three key presses](https://freesound.org/people/MattRuthSound/sounds/561664/) by [MattRuthSound](https://freesound.org/people/MattRuthSound/), licensed [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The high-quality MP3 preview was trimmed from 0.20 to 0.42 seconds, filtered at 80–3,200 Hz, slowed to 88% speed for a deeper thock, faded, peak-normalised and converted to mono 22,050 Hz PCM16 WAV. This is separate from the panel-button recording.
