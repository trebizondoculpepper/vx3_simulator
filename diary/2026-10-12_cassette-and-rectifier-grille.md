## Problem Statement

The west workbench needed a self-contained cassette recorder near the sink, with speakers and Play, Stop and Fast Forward controls. The shut plant-room door needed to reveal occasional blue flashes from the operating mercury-arc rectifier through its grille.

## Our Approach

Build an upright 1980s cassette player at portable equipment scale on intact worktop north of the basin. Give its transport visible state and reel motion, while leaving program audio for a future recording. Put the rectifier effect on surfaces behind the grille bars and gate it on rectifier power and door closure.

## Implementation

The player has a silver upright case, two large front speaker grilles, a central cassette door, a radio scale, a carry handle, spinning hubs, a status lamp and three clickable transport keys with button sounds. The plant door now briefly lights its grille blue at irregular intervals while the rectifier is powered and the door is closed. Reduced motion suppresses the flashes.

## Outcomes

The workbench inspection view shows the recorder beside the sink, clear of the basin. The plant-room inspection view shows the grille in its dark state while power is off.

## Next Steps

Recorded program content can be attached to the recorder later.
