# Development Diary Entry - 2026-10-13

## Context

- **Date**: 2026-10-13
- **Branch**: main
- **Contributors**: creative lead agent and owner

## Problem Statement

The owner preferred the earlier scientist-led box art and asked for two scientists working together, authentic 1990s packaging language, front and back panels for two designs, in-game screenshots on the backs, and fictional PC and Commodore Amiga availability.

## Our Approach

Used the existing alternate and primary covers as separate visual starting points. Generated two front panels with collaborating scientists and two matched back panels using the game's existing screenshots. Kept the original concepts intact for comparison.

## Outcomes

Four new flat packaging panels are in `art/marketing/`. The backs describe operating the rig, reading its instruments and exploring the laboratory in earnest period-style copy, with the requested PC and Commodore Amiga line.
