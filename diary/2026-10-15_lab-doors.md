# Development Diary Entry - 2026-10-15

## Context

- **Date**: 2026-10-15
- **Branch**: main
- **Contributors**: owner and Codex

## Problem Statement

The laboratory entrance needed better surface detail, brass push plates and suitable hazard signs. Door leaves appeared paper thin. The museum spotlight switch incorrectly played a fluorescent startup recording.

## Our Approach

Keep the existing paired-leaf operation and add real thickness, code-drawn material maps and physical hardware, preserving each face’s room lighting ownership.

## Implementation

The leaves are 50 mm thick. Paint, brass and diamond wired glass have deterministic procedural textures. Glazing has raised beads and gaskets; brass push plates have screw heads and a DRÜCKEN legend. Cylindrical pull handles are inside the lab; brass push plates face the corridor, matching the inward swing. Added hinge barrels, lower panel detail and German high-voltage and magnetic-field notices with restricted-access text. Updated the closed-door collision depth. Added `lab-doors` and `lab-doors-outside` inspection views. Removed the museum switch’s delayed ballast-start cue while retaining its Bakelite click.

## Outcomes

Typecheck, lint, formatting and production build pass. Verified both door faces in separate browser test tabs and operated the paired doors with E to inspect their solid edges. The owner’s existing game was not reloaded. Proof images are under `doc/design/screenshots/2026-10-15/`.
